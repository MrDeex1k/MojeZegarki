## App Store description

Your first automatic, a gift for a special occasion, a favorite everyday watch. My Watches brings their stories together in one place — on your iPhone.

YOUR COLLECTION
Add watches and photos, choose a main photo, and record reference numbers, purchase details and notes. Search by brand or model. Move sold watches to the Archive while keeping their history.

WHAT'S ON YOUR WRIST TODAY?
Log today's watch with a tap. Open Wearing to browse your history, add past days and filter entries by watch. You can log more than one watch per day.

ROOM FOR YOUR NEXT WATCH
Save watches to your wishlist with a photo, target price, link and priority. Once you buy a watch, move it into your collection.

DOCUMENTS WITH YOUR WATCHES
Attach PDF and image documents to individual watches. Find purchase information and important files alongside your photos and notes.

PRIVATE AND PERSONAL
Your collection works locally, without an account or internet connection. There are no ads or third-party analytics. Optionally protect access with Face ID or your device passcode.

Available in English and Polish, with light and dark appearances. Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The current version does not offer cross-device sync or full collection export and import.

## TestFlight beta description

Version 0.8.0 is a pre-release build of My Watches, a private watch collection app. Try photos, purchase details, documents, wear history and the wishlist. The app works locally without an account, ads, subscriptions or sync. English and Polish are supported. This beta precedes the first App Store release.

## What to Test — 0.8.0 (1)

1. Add a watch and a photo, edit its details, then close and reopen the app to check persistence.
2. Log Wearing today, add a past day and check that the same watch cannot be logged twice on one day.
3. Add a wishlist item, cancel moving it to the collection, then repeat and save.
4. Attach a PDF and an image document. Check preview, renaming and deletion.
5. Check Face ID/passcode protection, cancelled authentication and collection privacy when the app enters the background.
6. Try airplane mode, light/dark appearance, larger text and VoiceOver.
7. Archive and restore a sample watch. Use disposable data when checking permanent deletion.

Use sample photos and documents. Do not keep the only copy of important data in this beta: collection export and sync are not available.

Send feedback through TestFlight or to mywatches@mail.batycki.dev. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version/build, reproduction steps and expected result. Remove private information from attached screenshots.
